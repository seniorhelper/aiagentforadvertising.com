The short story

Why an ad agency started coding agents

Zach started Eye To Ad Media in Denver in 2012, doing search engine optimization and paid advertising for local businesses. Like everyone who runs ad accounts, he spent a big share of every week on the same chores: checking budgets, cleaning search terms, chasing leads, rebuilding the same report. You can read more about the agency at Eye To Ad Media's main site.

When language models became good enough to read messy data and write a decent first draft, the opportunity was obvious: let software handle the repetitive part and keep people on the decisions. The philosophy has not changed since: you dream it, we code it and make it real. Don't want to do the emails? Solved. Don't want to update that spreadsheet again? An agent can do it around the clock while you sleep.

Just as important is what it does not mean. These agents do not replace you or your team. They are tools that make a business more efficient and give people time back for the work they love, their families and their lives. People approve what matters.

Built on our own work first

The first advertising agents are being built this season for Eye To Ad Media's own operations: directory-submission triage, monthly SEO and ads report drafts, inbox triage and quote follow-ups. That way every rough edge is found on our accounts before it reaches yours. When those agents have real track records, we will say what they did, honestly and with permission.

Assistants we built and run every day

Before advertising agents, Zach coded the on-site assistants below for sites in the Eye To Ad Media network. Several are rule-based, scripted assistants rather than AI agents, and we describe them that way. They show we build, ship and maintain this kind of software; they are not client results.

  • Iriseyetoad.com

    The growth assistant on the Eye To Ad Media site, coded in-house and kept up to date as the site changes.

  • Scoutsearchconverts.com

    A scripted, rule-based assistant that greets visitors on that site and helps them find their way.

  • Rivitadvertisingforcontractors.com

    The on-site assistant for a site aimed at contractors, built and maintained by Zach.

  • Coraconversationalaibot.com

    A rule-based chatbot whose every reply was written by a person; it says so openly.

  • Lumi and Orbitallofus.one

    Two scripted guides that help visitors get around the site and can be tucked away.

  • Paxworldvrmall.com

    A mall concierge that helps visitors move around a virtual mall.

Site names are listed for reference only. We do not publish client logos, testimonials or case-study numbers we cannot back up.
