Privacy policy

In brief: the only personal details this site receives are the ones you choose to send through the contact form, and we use them to answer you.

Last updated October 10, 2026.

Who we are

aiagentforadvertising.com is run by Eye To Ad Media, based in Denver, Colorado. Contact: info (at) eyetoad.com or 1-800-481-8638.

Information you send us

When you send the contact form, we receive your name, email, optional phone number, business name, your description of the task and the ad platforms you checked. We do not add analytics trackers, advertising pixels or marketing cookies to this site.

How the form is processed

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox through FormSubmit, an outside form-delivery provider, which also sends you an automatic confirmation email. FormSubmit processes the submission to deliver it; see its own terms for how it handles data. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives standard request information such as your IP address.

How we use it

Only to reply to you, schedule the call you asked for, and keep a record of our conversation. We do not sell or rent your information, and we never subscribe you to newsletters without asking.

Data from agent projects

If you become a client, access to your ad accounts and business data is covered by the written blueprint for your project: what the agent may read, what it may change, where logs are kept and how access is removed.

Your choices

Email us to see, correct or delete the information you sent. We will confirm once it is done.
